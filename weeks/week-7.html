Week 7 homework · skill day

Your Practice

Light week. The retest is next, so this one is movement and fun rather than a grind. Rest the hard pulling so the retest is honest.

Pick your level

Pick the level that matches where you are, block by block — you might be a 4 on pull and a 6 on legs. If in doubt, take the easier one and do it properly.

The one to remember

Rest, sleep and food matter more than an extra session this week. Arrive fresh.

Getting the best out of it

Nothing to failure. Keep the daily hang, do the shapes for fun, and turn up next week rested.

One or two easy sessions

Fifteen minutes is plenty. Skip one entirely if you feel beaten up.

No warm-up shortcuts though — light sessions still need warm wrists and shoulders.

Each session

Movement and swing · one easy push · one easy hold.

Stop every set while it still feels fast and clean.

Every day

Hang from something. A bar, a door frame, a beam — ten to thirty seconds, once or twice.

It costs nothing, and this one habit is worth more than the rest put together.
