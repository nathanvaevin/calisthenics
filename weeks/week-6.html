Week 6 homework · core

Your Practice

Core week. Not sit-ups — the shapes that hold every other position together. This is the week the hollow body finally makes sense, because it is under your pull-up and your handstand too.

Pick your level

Pick the level that matches where you are, block by block — you might be a 4 on pull and a 6 on legs. If in doubt, take the easier one and do it properly.

The one to remember

A shaky clean thirty seconds beats a long sloppy minute. When the shape breaks, the set is over.

Getting the best out of it

Short, frequent and never sloppy. One hollow hold a day, even for a minute, is worth more than one long session.

Two short sessions

Fifteen to twenty minutes each. Any two days with a day between them.

Warm up for three minutes first: wrists, shoulders, hips, twenty squats, one hang.

Each session

Core first · one pull · one push · one legs.

Two core exercises at every level: one hold, one that moves.

Every day

One hollow hold, even for a minute, plus the daily hang.

It is the shape under your pull-up and your handstand too.
