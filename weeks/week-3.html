Week 3 homework · handstands

Your Practice

Upside down this week. The handstand is one line — wrist to shoulder to ribs to hips — and this week builds it. The pull keeps running underneath, because it always does.

Pick your level

Pick the level that matches where you are, block by block — you might be a 4 on pull and a 6 on legs. If in doubt, take the easier one and do it properly.

The one to remember

Push the floor away. A handstand is an overhead press you hold instead of finish.

Getting the best out of it

Balance work wants frequency, not duration. Ten focused minutes beats one long session where the wrists give out early.

Two short sessions

Fifteen to twenty minutes each. Any two days with a day between them.

Warm up for three minutes first: wrists, shoulders, hips, twenty squats, one hang.

Each session

The handstand block in full · then one pull · one push · one hold.

Short on time? The handstand block is the one that matters this week. Never start upside down cold.

Every day

Hang from something. A bar, a door frame, a beam — ten to thirty seconds, once or twice.

It costs nothing, and this one habit is worth more than the rest put together.
