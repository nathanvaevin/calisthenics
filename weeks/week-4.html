Week 4 homework · push-ups

Your Practice

Pressing week. Two push sessions, built so the second exercise adds volume without grinding the first one into the ground. The pull stays in to keep the goal alive.

Pick your level

Pick the level that matches where you are, block by block — you might be a 4 on pull and a 6 on legs. If in doubt, take the easier one and do it properly.

The one to remember

Elbows back, not flared. The body travels as one plank, from heel to head.

Getting the best out of it

Stop two reps before failure, every set. You are here twice this week, not once.

Two short sessions

Fifteen to twenty minutes each. Any two days with a day between them.

Warm up for three minutes first: wrists, shoulders, hips, twenty squats, one hang.

Each session

Push first · one pull · one legs · one hold.

Two pushing exercises at every level, the second one lighter than the first.

Every day

Hang from something. A bar, a door frame, a beam — ten to thirty seconds, once or twice.

It costs nothing, and this one habit is worth more than the rest put together.

The number on each exercise is where it sits in the skill tree. Tap any exercise to see how it is done and which muscles it works. If something here is confusing, ask me before you guess.
