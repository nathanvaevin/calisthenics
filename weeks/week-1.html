Week 1 practice · between class 1 and class 2

Your Practice

Everything here is built around the level you ticked on your scorecard. Pick your level, do the two short sessions, and hang from something every day. That is the whole plan.

Pick your level

Different tests, different levels: if you were a 4 on pull-ups and a 6 on squats, pick each block separately using the numbers on your card.

Getting the best out of it

Two sessions is the plan, but do not let that stop you. Even one day this week is already a real week of training, and it beats waiting until you have time for the perfect one.

Two sessions a week

Fifteen to twenty minutes each. Any two days with a day between them.

Warm up for three minutes first: wrists, shoulders, hips, twenty squats, one hang.

Each session

One pull · one push · one legs or core · one hold. Four things, that is it.

Alternate: session A takes the first option of each block, session B takes the second.

Every day

Hang from a bar or a door frame. Ten to thirty seconds, once or twice.

Costs nothing, builds the grip and shoulders that limit everyone.

Bring the sheet to class, or just the numbers. If something here is confusing, ask me before you guess.
