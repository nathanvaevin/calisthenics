Every skill breaks down the same way. Pick a track, open the ladder, work the rungs from the bottom up. The number in each circle is the level — the same 1–10 scale as your scorecard, so you can read straight across from the weekly sheets.

Level:

Levels are calibrated against the weekly sheets: 4 is where the course starts (negatives, active hangs, incline push-ups), 5 is the step up, 6 is the top of what we train now. Below 4 is pre-course, 7 and up is where the course goes next.

Hold and rep targets are working guidelines, not gospel — leverage, limb length and bodyweight all move the numbers. Move up a rung only when the current one feels owned: clean form, full control, no shaking through it.

Print test sheet gives you one page per group with a tick box per exercise — hand it out in week 1 and again in week 8.

To change a level, edit the lv: number on that rung in assets/engine.js. The weekly sheets read from the same file, so the numbers stay in step.
