Two of you, two focuses. Both lists are pain-free and neither needs a wall spotter. Find your block below and do it daily — these are short on purpose.

Pick your level

Getting the best out of it

Neither of these is a workout you need to psych up for. They are short daily habits, and they work because they are daily.

Daily, not weekly

Both lists are built to be done every day. Five to ten minutes is enough.

A missed day is fine. Two missed weeks is what undoes it.

Feel it, do not count it

The point is to feel each position, not to rack up reps.

Head-to-toe checklist on every hold: ribs down, pelvis tucked, shoulders pushing tall.

Nothing should hurt

A stretch or a light burn is fine. Anything sharp means stop.

Still hurting in a couple of weeks, or hurting at rest? See a physio.
