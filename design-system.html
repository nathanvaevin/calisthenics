13 The six rules

Do not

Nest bordered boxes. Maximum one card level. Depth comes from surface lightness and spacing.

Do not

Use mid tone navy as the base. Near black makes content separate.

Do not

Put amber on values, labels and headings. One amber per screen.

Do not

Set headings in letterspaced mono. Mono is for timers, counts and at most two labels.

Do not

Use a left label column with content beside it. On 390px it halves the usable width. Stack it.

Always

One primary action per screen, full width, bottom, 56px, black on amber.

Know your level. Choose your goal. Master what comes next.
